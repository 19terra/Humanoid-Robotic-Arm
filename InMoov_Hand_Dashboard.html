from cvzone.HandTrackingModule import HandDetector

detector = HandDetector(maxHands=1, detectionCon=0.8)
mySerial = cvzone.SerialObject("COM3", 9600, digits=1)

# In main loop:

    fingers = detector.fingersUp(hands[0])

Adhesives, Tendons & Lubricants Compatibility Guide

🧪 ABS Plastic Adhesives

Pure Acetone or Zap-A-Gap melts ABS surfaces together for a permanent chemical weld. Use pliers to hold parts tight.

🧪 PLA Plastic Adhesives

Use Zap-A-Gap (CA glue) or 2-Part Epoxy. Acetone will NOT weld PLA plastic.

🎣 Tendon Lines

Use 200LB Braided Fishing Line only. Never use nylon monofilament line because nylon stretches under load.

🧴 Gear Lubrication

Use White Silicone Grease between rotating wrist gears. Petroleum greases turn printed parts yellow over time.
